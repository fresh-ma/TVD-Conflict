\section{Limitations}

Our study covers English-language AdvBench goals, ten jailbreak mechanisms,
three judge backbones, and two evaluation protocols; the measured conflict and
failure-mode rates are not prevalence estimates beyond this setting.
Disagreement is a collection signal rather than ground truth, so identifying
the erroneous verdict still requires inspecting the interaction and rubric.
Because \textsc{TVD}-Conflict searches near judge decision boundaries, some
distribution shift may remain despite its realism controls. Broader prevalence
claims require validation across languages, domains, prompts, and judge
families.
