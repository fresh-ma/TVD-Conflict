\section{Limitations}

Our study covers English-language AdvBench goals, ten jailbreak mechanisms,
three judge backbones, and two evaluation protocols; its conflict rates and
failure-mode frequencies are not prevalence estimates for other domains or
deployed traffic. Disagreement is also a collection signal rather than ground
truth: identifying the erroneous verdict still requires inspecting the
interaction and rubric. Finally, \textsc{TVD}-Conflict deliberately searches
near judge decision boundaries. Its realism controls reduce, but cannot
eliminate, distribution shift from naturally occurring model responses.
Broader prevalence claims require validation across languages, domains,
prompts, and judge families.
