\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Reliability of LLM-as-a-Judge.}
Prior work has shown that model-based evaluators are susceptible to systematic biases. \citet{ICLR2025_fdca08d3} identify 12 types of bias in LLM-as-a-Judge and introduce CALM, a framework that uses automated, principle-guided modifications to quantify judge sensitivity across models. Their study evaluates predefined bias categories and consistency under controlled perturbations. Our work is complementary: rather than measuring a fixed set of biases within individual judges, TVD-Conflict searches for responses that induce conflicting safety decisions across multiple judges operating under the same evaluation criteria.
