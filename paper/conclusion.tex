\section{Conclusion}

In this work, we study disagreement among LLM-based jailbreak judges and
introduce \textsc{TVD}-Conflict, an agentic framework that uses disagreement as
feedback to discover evaluator-sensitive interactions. Conflicts are
structured across both different judge backbones and repeated evaluations of
the same backbone. Combining 1,702 pairs collected by \textsc{TVD}-Conflict
with attack-derived cases yields four primary failure modes and two
cross-cutting defects, establishing disagreement as a practical signal for
diagnostic stress testing.
